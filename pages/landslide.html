Landslide Risk Scale

Risk is estimated from rainfall accumulation, rainfall intensity and continuous rainfall duration.

Low

Low rainfall intensity and relatively short rainfall duration.

Moderate

Increased rainfall accumulation or prolonged rainfall requires monitoring.

High

Heavy rainfall combined with prolonged rainfall indicates potentially hazardous conditions.

Critical

Severe rainfall conditions and prolonged rainfall indicate a significantly elevated risk.
