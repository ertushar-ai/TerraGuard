Earthquake Risk Assessment

TerraGuard estimates earthquake risk using event magnitude, distance from the selected location, earthquake depth, and recent nearby earthquake activity.

Safe

No significant nearby earthquake activity detected.

Low

Lower-risk earthquake activity has been detected in the monitoring area.

Moderate

A significant nearby event or recent activity requires monitoring.

High / Critical

Strong and/or nearby earthquake activity may require immediate attention.

Note: This is a project-level risk estimate based on available earthquake data. It is not an official earthquake warning or prediction.

How It Works

1

Select Location

TerraGuard uses the selected monitoring location and its geographic coordinates.

2

Fetch USGS Data

Recent earthquake events are retrieved from the live USGS earthquake catalogue.

3

Calculate Distance

TerraGuard calculates the distance between each earthquake and the selected location.

4

Analyze Earthquake

Magnitude, distance, depth and recent earthquake activity are analyzed together.

5

Determine Risk

The combined factors are converted into a TerraGuard risk level from Safe to Critical.

Data Source

Earthquake information is obtained from the United States Geological Survey (USGS) earthquake catalogue. TerraGuard uses live GeoJSON earthquake data to analyze recent events around the selected monitoring location.
